4. Territory System

Territories are real-world locations you can capture and own. Build your empire by controlling the streets!

How Territories Work

  • Size: Each territory is 338m × 338m
  • Names: 1,600+ unique procedurally generated names
  • Colors: Unclaimed = Gray, Owned = Player's unique color

Capturing Territories

  1. Move within range (~100m) of an unclaimed territory
  2. Tap the territory marker on the map
  3. Fight the territory boss (NPC defender)
  4. Win to claim ownership

Territory Benefits

Benefit Description
Passive Income~$30 per territory over time
XP Reward+50 XP on capture
Reputation+10 Rep on capture
Visual OwnershipYour color & avatar displayed on map

Challenging Owned Territories (PvP)

If a territory is owned by another player, you fight their stats instead of an NPC boss. The winner takes ownership of the territory.

5. Battle System

Combat in Street Legends is turn-based. Defeat enemies to earn XP, cash, and loot!

Starting a Battle

  • Find enemy markers (skulls) on the map
  • Enemies spawn based on your level (±1 level range)
  • Tap enemy → View stats → Press "FIGHT"

Combat Actions

Action Description Cost
AttackBasic attack using your Attack statFree
SkillUse equipped skill for special effectsEnergy
DefendReduce incoming damage by 50%Free
FleeEscape battle (may fail)Free

Combat Mechanics

Mechanic Formula
Base DamageAttack - (Enemy Defense ÷ 2)
Miss Chance10% base, reduced by Luck (min 5%)
Crit Chance5% base + (Luck × 1%)
Crit Damage2x normal damage
Skill MissOnly 5% chance

Battle Rewards

Outcome Rewards
VictoryXP, Cash, possible item drop, Reputation
DefeatLose some HP, no rewards
FleeNo rewards, escape to safety

Enemy Stats Formula

  • Health = (80 + Level × 18) × Boss Multiplier
  • Attack = (8 + Level × 3.2) × Boss Multiplier
  • Defense = (5 + Level × 1.8) × Boss Multiplier
  • Boss Multiplier = 1.3 (bosses), 1.0 (regular)

7. Bank Robbery System

High risk, high reward! Rob banks for big cash payouts.

  • Find bank markers (🏦) on the map
  • Rob the bank for a 50/50 chance heist
  • Success: Big cash reward
  • Failure: Lose some cash and take damage
