Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Street Legends! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our GPS-based RPG mobile application.

By using Street Legends, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

1. Location Data

Street Legends uses your device's location to power core gameplay features including:

  • Discovering and capturing territories on the map
  • Finding nearby missions, enemies, and points of interest
  • Tracking fitness activities (step counting) for in-game rewards

Location data is collected only while you are actively using the app or when background location is enabled for fitness tracking features you have opted into.

2. Profile Pictures

If you choose to upload a profile picture for your character, we store this image on our secure servers. Profile pictures may be visible to other players when you capture territories or appear on leaderboards.

3. Account Information

When you create a character, we collect:

  • Username/Character name
  • Character customization data (stats, skills, inventory)
  • Game progress and achievements
  • Device identifier for account association

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the game experience
  • To enable location-based gameplay features
  • To save your game progress and character data
  • To display your profile to other players in multiplayer features
  • To track fitness activities and award in-game rewards
  • To improve and optimize the app experience

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

Your data is never sold, rented, or traded to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties for their marketing purposes.

We may share limited information only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting), under strict confidentiality agreements
  • If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users
  • Aggregated, anonymized data for analytics (which cannot identify you personally)

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure cloud storage infrastructure
  • Regular security assessments

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please contact us or use the in-app account deletion feature.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Disable location services through your device settings
  • Opt out of fitness tracking features

Children's Privacy

Street Legends is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the app's support feature or reach out to our development team.
