<original_problem_statement>
The user wants to build a Grand Theft Auto (GTA) style GPS RPG game.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS (from initial state and this session):**
- **Core Gameplay:** Monster battles and missions triggered by GPS location.
- **Map & Theme:** A real-world map (using OpenStreetMap) that shows the player's location, enemies, missions, and dynamic shops.
- **GPS Movement:** The player's character on the map must move in real-time as the player moves in the real world.
- **Character System:** A character screen showing stats, skills, inventory, and equipable items. Players should be able to upload a custom profile picture, sell/delete items, and view their owned territories.
- **Authentication:** Players must be able to create an account (login/password) to save their character, inventory, and game progress.
- **Economy:** An item shop, item drops from battles, and dynamic, location-based gun shops on the map.
- **Multiplayer & Territories:** PvP, Guilds, Leaderboards, and a Territory control system.
    - Territories should be a grid of equal-sized squares that can be captured. They start as one color and change to the owner's color.
    - Player profile pictures should be displayed on owned territories, on the leaderboard, and next to the player's name.
- **Progression & Content:**
    - A 10-tier enemy system, where players only encounter enemies from their current and previous tiers.
    - A passive health regeneration system (25% every 15 minutes).
    - A fitness tracker page for steps, calories, and distance, with rewards for hitting goals.
    - A revamped mission system:
        - Phone Booth missions with goals like defeating monsters, walking steps, or traveling distance.
        - Bounty missions (dollar sign icon) for defeating specific types of monsters.
- **UI/UX:**
    - Defeated monsters and completed missions must disappear from the map.
    - The list of nearby entities at the bottom of the map screen should be removed.
</original_problem_statement>
